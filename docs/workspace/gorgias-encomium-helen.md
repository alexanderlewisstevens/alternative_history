## Why This Text Matters

This is a compact starting point for the project because it refuses to treat speech as harmless. It asks whether language can compel, excuse, enchant, reorder blame, and move judgment.

For a history project, that matters because historical narratives also persuade. They assign blame, authorize memory, and make some possibilities feel natural.

## Working Summary

The text defends Helen by shifting attention from moral blame to the forces that can overwhelm judgment. Speech becomes one of those forces. That makes the piece useful for thinking about rhetoric as action, not decoration.
