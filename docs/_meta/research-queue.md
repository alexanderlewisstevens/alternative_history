## Open Questions

- What is the first theoretical spine: rhetoric and poetics, archive and memory, empire and critique, or another path?
- Which thinkers are central enough to organize reading paths rather than appear as isolated entries?
- Should speculative alternate-history fiction be included, or should the project focus on historical method, theory, criticism, and public memory?
- Should entries be short encyclopedia-style pieces, long essays, or both?
- What reading level should the public site target?

- Interpretation, allegory, and commentary.
- The archive as power.
- Empire, representation, and criticism.
- Translation as a second authorship.
