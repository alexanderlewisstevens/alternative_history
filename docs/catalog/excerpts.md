# Excerpts

Excerpt entries are short, rights-reviewed passages paired with commentary.

Do not add copied text here until the source has a clear rights basis. When in doubt, summarize the source and leave the excerpt field empty.

Prefer excerpts that help a constellation come alive. A passage should show a relation, tension, inheritance, refusal, or transformation, not merely stand as a memorable quotation.
